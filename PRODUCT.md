## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user choice, 2026-10-02). Published on GitHub Pages as its own repo (APXWeb/intercientifica-proposta-2), separate from Proposta 1 (APXWeb/intercientifica). Libraries only from CDN and only where they earn it.

## Users

- Laboratory professionals and technical leads (responsáveis técnicos) in neonatal and prenatal screening programs, looking for a specific kit, its reference, presentation and assay format.
- Lab managers, public health programs, institutions and commercial partners evaluating the company as a supplier.
- Researchers looking for the company's scientific publications.

## Product Purpose

Institutional and commercial website of Intercientifica (São José dos Campos, SP, founded 1994): a Brazilian manufacturer of kits, reagents, software and personalized collection cards for neonatal and prenatal screening. The site must make the two kit lines understandable, prove credibility (ANVISA GMP, external quality programs, publications) and route visitors to the scientific or commercial team.

This is APX's second proposal for the client, shown alongside Proposta 1 (https://apxweb.github.io/intercientifica/). It must stand on its own concept.

## Positioning

National manufacturing (100% nacional) of screening kits since 1994; NeoMAP® multiplex kits on the Luminex® xMAP® platform (several analytes from one sample in one assay) and NeoLISA® enzymatic/colorimetric kits; automation with Hamilton® Robotics and Opentrons® systems.

## Product Principles

1. Technical accuracy over spectacle: never change the meaning of a product claim.
2. A lab professional must reach a kit's reference and presentation in two moves.
3. Every number shown carries its source context.
4. Science-as-imagery is abstract and labeled as such, never presented as a real Intercientifica process.
